Cosmic Shore — systems I own

Cosmic Shore is the game I work on at Froglet. These are the systems in it that are mine.

The UI, rebuilt

I redid the game’s entire UI, the architecture and the look. The ship HUD moved onto subscriptions: 4500bd949 cut ShipHUDController by ~300 lines into HudSubscriptionSO and per-ability views, and 662e300ed then deleted the superseded HUD scripts. On top of that, a new visual language across every screen: menus, the hangar, the arcade flow, the event feed and toasts.

Backend: PlayFab to UGS

I moved the game’s whole backend onto Unity Gaming Services and took the old one out. Anonymous sign-in and player profiles (#93), one UGSDataService as the single entry point for cloud save and data (#346), profiles and avatars on a live PlayerDataService across every menu (#136 #537), leaderboards, Cloud Code, and Unity IAP with web checkout (#405). PlayFab then came out entirely, after proving it was dead.

Data-driven cameras

A custom camera controller, CameraSettingsSO assets and an interface-based CameraManager, replacing ad-hoc follow logic. Trailer and cinematic cameras sit on top.

Analytics and consent

I retired Firebase for one facade behind a consent and age gate, fanning out to UGS and a PostHog sink batched through an on-disk queue. Debounced cloud saves, per-vessel tracking and UGS stat keys.

Tooling inside the project

Central logging: CSDebug, with per-type flags and a tabbed control window, replacing raw Debug.Log across the codebase (#102 #530). A performance benchmark tool with a CPU/GPU split and bound-verdict overlays (#529 #544). A dialogue system driven by SOAP events (#434). The Unity 6.3 LTS migration (#555).
